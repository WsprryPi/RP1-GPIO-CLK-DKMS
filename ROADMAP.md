# Maintenance scope

RP1-GPIO-CLK-DKMS maintenance covers bounded UAPI behavior, stock-kernel resource
ownership, route-neutral packaging, single-route administration, cancellation,
and attributable recovery. Deterministic host tests and disposable-container
regressions cover these interfaces and lifecycle operations.

Source, UAPI, package, and runtime identities are validated independently of
WsprryPi publication and qualification. Each target, kernel, route, waveform,
and RF qualification claim requires its own scoped evidence. WsprryPi owns
production provider selection and product acceptance.
